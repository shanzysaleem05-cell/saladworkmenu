Finding a fast-casual restaurant that caters to children while still offering healthy, whole-food options can be a challenge. Thankfully, the [SaladWorks Menu](/menu) features a dedicated "Kids Works" section designed to please picky eaters and budget-conscious parents alike.

## The Kids Works Menu

Everything on the Kids Works menu is priced under $8.00, making it an incredibly affordable option for family dining. Here is a breakdown of what you can expect:

### 1. Kids Bento Box
*   **Price:** ~$7.99
*   **Details:** The [Kids Bento Box](/menu/kids-bento-box) is perfect for children who like their food separated. It features compartments with fresh veggies, a protein, fruit, and a treat.

### 2. Kids Create Your Own Salad
*   **Price:** ~$6.99
*   **Details:** Does your child love salads? The [Kids Create Your Own Salad](/menu/kids-create-your-own-salad) is a smaller portion of the adult version, allowing them to pick their favorite fresh ingredients.

### 3. Kids Grilled Cheese
*   **Price:** ~$6.49
*   **Details:** A classic staple. The [Kids Grilled Cheese](/menu/kids-grilled-cheese) is simple, comforting, and guaranteed to be eaten. 

### 4. Kids Grilled Chicken Quesadilla
*   **Price:** ~$7.49
*   **Details:** For a protein-packed lunch, the [Kids Grilled Chicken Quesadilla](/menu/kids-grilled-chicken-quesadilla) is a crowd-pleaser that sneaks in high-quality grilled chicken.

## What Else is Included?

All Kids Works meals come with a side (typically an apple or chips) and a kid-sized beverage. This complete meal package ensures you aren't nickel-and-dimed at the register. If your family is craving a healthy lunch out, SaladWorks provides an excellent, budget-friendly experience.
