When it comes to fast-casual dining, few places make it as easy to track your macros as SaladWorks. Because their menu is so highly customizable, you have total control over your calorie intake. In this guide, our editorial team breaks down the nutritional profile of the [SaladWorks Menu](/menu) to help you make the best dietary choices.

## Lowest Calorie Options

If you are strictly monitoring your caloric intake, building a custom salad is your safest bet. However, if you prefer to order from the Signature Salads menu, here are the lowest calorie options:
*   **Bently Salad:** Often clocking in under 350 calories (before dressing), the [Bently](/menu/bently) is a fantastic low-calorie, high-flavor option.
*   **Mediterranean Chicken:** The [Mediterranean Chicken](/menu/mediterranean-chicken) salad offers a robust flavor profile with feta and olives, yet remains relatively light.

## High Protein, Muscle-Building Salads

For those looking to fuel a workout or stay full throughout the day, protein is key. 
*   **Steakhouse Salad:** The [Steakhouse Salad](/menu/steakhouse-salad) is packed with fire-grilled steak and bleu cheese, easily providing over 30g of protein.
*   **Buffalo Bleu Wrap:** Don't underestimate the wraps! The [Buffalo Bleu Wrap](/menu/buffalo-bleu-wrap) is loaded with grilled chicken and spicy buffalo sauce, making it an excellent high-protein lunch on the go.

## Navigating the Dressings

The biggest hidden calorie trap at any salad bar is the dressing. At SaladWorks, dressings can add anywhere from 50 to 300 calories to your meal.
*   **Lighter Choices:** Opt for the Balsamic Vinaigrette or Lite Raspberry Vinaigrette to keep calories low.
*   **Indulgent Choices:** The Green Goddess and Chipotle Ranch are delicious but calorie-dense. Consider asking for these "on the side" so you can control your portion.

Remember, the nutritional values provided are estimates. For the most precise and up-to-date information, including allergen data, always consult your local SaladWorks restaurant.
