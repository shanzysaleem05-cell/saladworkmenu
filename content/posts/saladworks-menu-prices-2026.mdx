Welcome to our comprehensive guide on **SaladWorks menu prices for 2026**. If you're looking for fresh, fast-casual dining that won't break the bank, you're in the right place. Our editorial team has gathered data from across the country to bring you the most accurate national averages.

## Signature Salads: What to Expect

The core of the [SaladWorks Menu](/menu) revolves around their Signature Salads. In 2026, you can generally expect these to range from **$11.99 to $14.99**, depending on your location and choice of premium protein. 

For instance, the incredibly popular [Sophie's Salad](/menu/sophies) remains a fan favorite, typically priced around $11.99. It combines mixed greens, grilled chicken, bacon, Monterey Jack cheese, and more, making it a robust meal. Meanwhile, the [Steakhouse Salad](/menu/steakhouse-salad) leans toward the higher end of that price spectrum but delivers premium fire-grilled steak.

## Wraps, Melts, and Soups

Not in the mood for a salad? SaladWorks has you covered:
*   **Wraps:** Most wraps, like the [Buffalo Bleu Wrap](/menu/buffalo-bleu-wrap), sit comfortably in the $11.99 range. They are perfect for on-the-go lunches.
*   **Melts & Paninis:** Warm, toasted sandwiches like the [Steak & Cheddar Melt](/menu/steak-cheddar-melt) offer comfort food satisfaction for roughly $10.49.
*   **Soups:** A cup of [Broccoli Cheddar Soup](/menu/broccoli-cheddar-soup) makes an excellent side dish, typically costing around $5.49.

## How to Get the Best Value

If you want the best bang for your buck, we highly recommend the **Pick 2 Combo**. For about **$13.49**, you can pair a half salad with a cup of soup or a half sandwich. It's the perfect way to sample multiple menu items without overspending. Read our full [Pick 2 Combo Guide](/blog/saladworks-pick-2-combo-guide) for our top pairings.
