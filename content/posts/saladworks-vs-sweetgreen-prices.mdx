The fast-casual salad market is booming, but two names constantly dominate the conversation: SaladWorks and Sweetgreen. Both offer fresh, customizable bowls, but which one is better for your wallet? Our pricing analysts took a deep dive into the [SaladWorks Menu](/menu) and compared it head-to-head with Sweetgreen's offerings for 2026.

## Base Price Comparison

At a glance, SaladWorks positions itself as a slightly more accessible option. 
*   **SaladWorks Signature Salads:** Typically range from **$11.99 to $14.99**. For example, the [Southwest Chipotle Ranch](/menu/southwest-chipotle-ranch) is usually around $12.49.
*   **Sweetgreen Signature Bowls:** Generally start around **$12.95** and can easily exceed **$16.00** for premium bowls with steak or salmon.

## Customization and Add-Ons

Both chains allow you to build your own salad. 
SaladWorks offers the [Create Your Own Salad](/menu/half-create-your-own) starting at a competitive base price. They are generous with their standard toppings (you get up to 5 for free). 
Sweetgreen also offers a custom bowl, but their premium toppings (like avocado, roasted sweet potatoes, or goat cheese) tend to carry a higher upcharge, which can rapidly inflate the cost of your lunch.

## Menu Variety

Where SaladWorks truly shines in this comparison is variety. While Sweetgreen focuses almost exclusively on salads and warm bowls, SaladWorks offers a robust menu of [Wraps](/menu/wrap-or-grilled-wrap), [Melts](/menu/steak-cheddar-melt), and [Soups](/menu/soup). 

If you're dining with a group where not everyone wants a salad, SaladWorks is undoubtedly the better choice. Furthermore, their [Pick 2 Combo](/blog/saladworks-pick-2-combo-guide) offers unmatched value that Sweetgreen simply does not replicate.

**The Verdict:** If you want premium, hyper-seasonal ingredients, Sweetgreen is excellent. But for everyday value, larger portion sizes, and menu variety, SaladWorks wins the pricing war.
