If you've looked at the [SaladWorks Menu](/menu) and found yourself unable to choose between a crisp salad, a warm melt, or a comforting soup, you are not alone. Luckily, SaladWorks offers the ultimate solution: the **Pick 2 Combo**. 

## What is the Pick 2 Combo?

For a base price of roughly **$13.49**, the Pick 2 allows you to select two "half" portions from the following categories:
1.  **Half Signature Salad**
2.  **Half Melt or Panini**
3.  **Cup of Soup**

It also comes with a freshly baked roll. This combo is widely regarded as the best value on the menu, allowing you to enjoy a variety of flavors and temperatures in one meal.

## Our Favorite Pairings

With dozens of possible combinations, it can be overwhelming to order. Here are the editorial team's top 3 favorite Pick 2 combos:

### 1. The Classic Comfort
*   **Pairing:** [Half Sophie's Salad](/menu/half-signature-salad) + [Cup of Chicken Noodle Soup](/menu/chicken-noodle-soup)
*   **Why it works:** The crisp, refreshing bite of the Sophie's Salad perfectly cuts through the savory, warming broth of the classic chicken noodle.

### 2. The Protein Powerhouse
*   **Pairing:** [Half Steakhouse Salad](/menu/half-signature-salad) + [Half Steak & Cheddar Melt](/menu/half-melt)
*   **Why it works:** Double the steak! This is a heavy, satisfying meal perfect for post-workout recovery or a large lunch appetite.

### 3. The Vegetarian Delight
*   **Pairing:** [Half Tofu Superfood Salad](/menu/half-signature-salad) + [Cup of Broccoli Cheddar Soup](/menu/broccoli-cheddar-soup)
*   **Why it works:** Nutrient-dense superfoods paired with rich, creamy cheese soup creates a perfectly balanced vegetarian meal.
