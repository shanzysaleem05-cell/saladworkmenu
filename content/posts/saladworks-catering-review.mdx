When organizing a corporate lunch, a family gathering, or a major event, finding a catering option that appeals to a variety of dietary needs can be a headache. That's where [SaladWorks Catering](/catering) shines. Our editorial team reviewed the 2026 catering options to see if they deliver on their promise of fresh, customizable group meals.

## The Catering Menu Breakdown

SaladWorks offers several different catering formats, ensuring there is a solution for both small office meetings and massive parties.

### The Signature Salad Bar
This is the crown jewel of SaladWorks catering. For a per-person price, you get a fully deconstructed salad bar delivered to your event. Guests can build their own versions of classics like the [Fuji Apple Crunch](/menu/fuji-apple-crunch) or create entirely custom bowls. It accommodates vegans, vegetarians, and meat-eaters simultaneously.

### Sandwich and Wrap Trays
If salads aren't enough, the wrap trays are an excellent addition. They typically feature an assortment of their most popular handhelds, such as the [Turkey Jalapeno Crunch Wrap](/menu/turkey-jalapeno-crunch-wrap) and the [Chicken Pesto](/menu/chicken-pesto) panini. 

### Boxed Lunches
For corporate environments where buffet-style isn't practical, Boxed Lunches are the perfect solution. Each box includes a half salad or sandwich, a side (like chips or an apple), and a cookie. 

## Pricing and Value

Catering prices vary heavily based on head count and location. However, on average, you can expect to spend between **$10 to $14 per person** depending on whether you opt for basic trays or the premium Salad Bar experience. Compared to other fast-casual chains, this represents excellent value, especially given the quality and freshness of the ingredients.
